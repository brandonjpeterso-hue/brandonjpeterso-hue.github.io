How this is calculated

The future value is a lump sum grown at the periodic rate, plus the future value of an annuity at the same rate. A monthly contribution is converted to an equivalent deposit per compounding period.

Periodic rate i = r / n, periods N = n × t.

Lump sum: P × (1 + i)ᴺ. Ordinary annuity: PMT × [((1 + i)ᴺ − 1) / i]. Annuity due multiplies that by (1 + i). If i = 0, growth is principal plus deposits.
