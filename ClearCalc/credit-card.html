Credit card payoff

Estimate how long a card balance takes to clear on a minimum-payment rule or a fixed monthly amount you choose.

Inputs

Payment rule
How this is calculated

Monthly interest is balance × APR / 12. A minimum payment is the greater of $25 or (selected % of the current balance + that month’s interest). If the payment is less than or equal to the interest charged, the balance does not decline. Results are capped at 60 years of payments.
