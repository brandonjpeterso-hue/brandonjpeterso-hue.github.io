Emergency fund

A cash target of 3, 6, or 12 months of expenses — or a custom length — and how long the gap takes to fill at a monthly deposit. No investment return is assumed.

Inputs

Target months
How this is calculated

Target is monthly expenses × months. The gap is target minus what you already have. Months to fill is the gap divided by your monthly deposit, rounded up. This treats the fund as cash — no yield, no inflation.
