Nest egg vs FIRE number

How this is calculated

FIRE number is annual spending ÷ withdrawal rate. At 4% that is spending × 25. Years to FIRE uses the same monthly compound math as the savings-goal calculator.

Coast FIRE is the amount that would grow to the FIRE number by the year you chose with no further contributions: FIRE number ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ. Markets, inflation, taxes, and sequence risk are not in this model.
