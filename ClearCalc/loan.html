How this is calculated

For a fixed-rate fully amortizing loan, M = P × [r (1 + r)ⁿ] / [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1] where r is the monthly rate and n is months. If the rate is 0%, M = P / n.

Each month, interest is charged on the remaining balance; the rest reduces principal. Taxes, insurance, fees, and variable rates are not included.
