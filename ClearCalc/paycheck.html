Retirement paycheck

Social Security, pension, and a portfolio withdrawal, next to what you plan to spend. The leftover is a monthly surplus or a gap.

Inputs

4% of the portfolio per year is the common starting point.
How this is calculated

Portfolio draw is portfolio × withdrawal rate ÷ 12. Total paycheck adds Social Security, pension, and other income. Extra portfolio needed is monthly gap × 12 ÷ withdrawal rate.
