Pension vs lump sum

Take the monthly pension, or invest the lump sum and withdraw that same check. Shows when the lump would run out, and what a survivor would be paid.

Inputs

0 if the check never increases.
How this is calculated

The lump is invested at your expected return. Each month it grows, then the pension amount is withdrawn. A COLA raises that withdrawal once a year. The age shown is when the lump hits $0.
