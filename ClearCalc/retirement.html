Balance over time

How this is calculated

Each year, your contribution is salary × contribution %, capped at the employee limit you enter. A 401(k) match is salary × min(your %, match-limit %) × match rate. Amounts are added monthly and compounded at the annual return divided by 12.

The 2026 elective deferral limit is $24,500 for 401(k)/403(b)/TSP and $7,500 for IRAs before catch-up. Limits change; this is not tax, vesting, or investment advice.
