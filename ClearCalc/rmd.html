Required minimum distribution

This year’s IRA or 401(k) required withdrawal from your age and last December’s balance. Uses the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table.

Inputs

The age you turn during the year of the withdrawal.
How this is calculated

Required amount is December 31 balance ÷ life-expectancy factor from the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table (2022 table). A spouse who is the sole beneficiary and more than 10 years younger uses a different joint table, which this calculator does not apply. This is an estimate, not tax advice.
