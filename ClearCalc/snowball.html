Snowball vs avalanche

Pay minimums on every debt, then send extra to the smallest balance (snowball) or the highest APR (avalanche). Compare months and interest.

Inputs

On top of all minimums, applied to one debt at a time.

Remaining balance

How this is calculated

Each month, interest is charged on every remaining balance, then each minimum is paid. Any leftover minimum plus your extra payment goes to one target debt: the smallest balance for snowball, or the highest APR for avalanche. When that debt is gone, leftover extra moves to the next target in the same month.

Avalanche usually costs less interest. Snowball can clear a small debt sooner. Fees, penalty APRs, and card-minimum formulas are not modeled.
