Social Security claiming

Compare a benefit taken at 62, at full retirement age, and at 70. Uses the standard early-claim reduction and delayed credits. No cost-of-living raises.

Inputs

The amount on your statement at full retirement age.
Used only for the lifetime total.
How this is calculated

Early claims are reduced by 5/9 of 1% per month for the first 36 months before full retirement age, then 5/12 of 1% per month. Waiting past full retirement age adds 2/3 of 1% per month until 70. Breakeven ignores inflation, taxes, and survivor benefits.
