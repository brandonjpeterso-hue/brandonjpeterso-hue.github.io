Survivor income

What monthly income is left if one of you dies. Social Security is simplified to the higher of the two checks. A pension continues only at the survivor percent you enter.

Inputs

How this is calculated

While both are living, income is the sum of both Social Security benefits, both pensions, and other income. After a death, Social Security is modeled as the larger of the two benefits only. Each pension continues at the survivor percent you entered.
